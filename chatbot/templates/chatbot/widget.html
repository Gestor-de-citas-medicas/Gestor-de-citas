How to Use MAMP

Your complete guide to the Medical Appointment Platform

👤 For Patients
🔍 Find a Doctor

Go to Find Doctor in the sidebar. Filter by specialty, name, or city. Click View Availability on any card to see open slots, then click Book.

🤖 Book via AI Assistant

Click the robot button (bottom-right corner). Type what you need, e.g. "I need a psychiatrist" or "Book me with a cardiologist for tomorrow". The assistant handles the rest.

📋 My Appointments

Go to My Appointments to track all your bookings. You can cancel pending ones or leave a review after completed visits.

🤖 AI Assistant Tips
  • Say what type of doctor you need (e.g. "psychiatrist", "cardiologist", "addiction specialist")
  • The assistant will list available doctors — pick one by name
  • Provide a date like "tomorrow" or "May 20th"
  • Confirm the slot when prompted and the booking is created instantly
  • Use 🗑️ to clear chat history and start a fresh conversation
